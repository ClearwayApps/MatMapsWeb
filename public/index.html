About MatMaps

One UK app for combat sports

MatMaps is the UK combat sports app for boxers, BJJ, Muay Thai, MMA, wrestling and more. Fighters find gyms and training. Club owners run their page, events and staff.

Location tools help users find relevant clubs nearby. Community reporting and in-app account deletion give users clear control over their experience and data.
