2. Plans and account roles

Features depend on account role and active entitlement. Free standard accounts can discover clubs and events and use the free features shown in the app, but do not receive a unique @username and cannot use friends, tagging or private messaging. Usage limits may apply to the training timer and notebook.

Athlete accounts may use eligible social, training and profile features. Club owners manage a club under an active club subscription. Club staff receive access through the owner's invitation and do not buy a separate staff subscription.

Feature availability may also depend on device permissions, platform support and whether app-store billing has been connected.

Eligible users choose a unique @username. A personal @username can normally be changed only once every six months. You are shown this restriction before confirming your first username.

A club @username is generated from the confirmed club name. It can normally be changed only once a year or where the club's official name has genuinely changed. We may adjust, reserve or remove a username that impersonates another person or club, infringes rights, misleads users, contains prohibited content or creates a security risk.

A username does not create ownership of a trade mark or other legal right.

4. Friends, tagging and private messages

Eligible users can search by @username, send and respond to friend requests and tag eligible users. If two users are friends, messages can enter the main chat list. A message from a non-friend enters Message Requests until the recipient accepts it.

A declined request does not start a conversation. Do not repeatedly contact, harass or use new accounts to reach someone who has declined or blocked you. Users can archive or delete their own chat view and can block another user, subject to the behaviour described in the app.

Users communicate as themselves. Club pages do not have a direct-message inbox and no user is entitled to message a club directly through MatMaps. Club contact remains through the email address, telephone number or website shown on the club page. A message button on a club notice starts a personal one-to-one chat with the owner or staff member who posted it.

5. Clubs, staff, affiliations and member notes

A club cannot be managed on the Free plan. Only the club owner can create, copy or cancel staff invitations and add or remove staff. Staff can join only through a valid one-time invitation link from the owner. An invitation can fail if it has expired, has already been used, was cancelled or the club has no remaining staff seat.

Depending on permissions, staff may edit club details, post as the club, add events and media, verify affiliated members, send club notices and add member notes. There can never be more than eight people in total running a club page: one owner and up to seven staff members.

A user's selection of an affiliated club can remain unverified until an owner or authorised staff member confirms it. Club owners and staff must use verification and coach-note tools responsibly, keep information accurate and confidential, and avoid recording another person's sensitive information without a proper reason and permission. Sending a note to a member shares that note with the member's account.

The club owner is responsible for the acts of staff they invite and for removing access promptly when it is no longer appropriate.
