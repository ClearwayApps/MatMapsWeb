1. Scope

This policy applies to the MatMaps Android and iOS applications, matmaps.co.uk and support interactions that link to this policy. It does not control independent club websites, social-media pages or other third-party services you choose to open.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and authentication: name, email address, sign-in provider identifiers, authentication records and account status. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile: display name, unique @username where available, profile photo, biography, sports, level or belt, affiliated club and subscription or role information.
  • Social content: Arena and Travelling Fighter Hub posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, saves, shares, tags, mentions and reports.
  • Connections and communications: friend requests and friendships, direct messages and message requests, blocks, unread status, club staff chat, club notices and related timestamps. Private messages are not end-to-end encrypted; do not send highly sensitive information.
  • Club operations: club claims, ownership and staff membership, one-time staff invitations, affiliations and verification decisions, events, club media, promotions, analytics and member or coach notes.
  • Training: timer settings, session durations, scores, tagged training partners, training logs, progress and notebook entries.
  • Directory information: club and event names, descriptions, locations, coordinates, contact details and media supplied by clubs or obtained from public club websites, social pages and directories.
  • Location: current or approximate location when you grant permission, used for nearby clubs, distances and location tags. We do not use location continuously in the background.
  • Purchases: product, entitlement, renewal and transaction status supplied by Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat. MatMaps does not receive full payment-card details.
  • Device and diagnostics: app version, operating system, device identifiers, IP address, notification token, language, crash, performance, security and usage information.
  • Support and moderation: enquiries, evidence, reports, reasons, decisions, appeals and actions needed to protect users or the service.
  • Advertising: where AdMob is enabled for free users, consent choices, advertising identifier where permitted, IP address, device information and ad interaction data.

You must provide essential account and authentication information to use account-only features. Other information is optional, although a feature may not work without the information it needs.

4. Who can see information

  • Profile information, approved club reviews, club listings, events and content posted to public feeds may be visible to other users and, where a public link exists, visitors.
  • Direct messages and message requests are visible to the participants. Club staff chats are visible only to authorised staff for that club.
  • Club notices are visible to the intended club members. Club affiliations and verification status may be visible as part of profile and club-member functions.
  • Coach notes remain within the authorised club/member workflow unless the author chooses to share a note with the member.
  • Reports, moderation records and support messages are restricted to authorised administrators and service providers who need them.

Anything you publish publicly may be copied or shared by others. Review content before posting and do not publish another person's private information without permission.

5. Location and device permissions

MatMaps asks for location only while you use relevant nearby features. You can deny or withdraw permission in device settings and still browse using manual search, although proximity ordering may be unavailable. A location tag you intentionally add to a post can be visible with that post.

Camera and microphone access is used only when you choose to capture media. The system photo picker is used when you choose existing media. Calendar access is used only when you choose to save an event. Notification permission is used for alerts you enable.

The in-app Discovery map uses OpenStreetMap-derived map tiles rather than the Google Maps SDK. Tile requests can disclose technical information such as your IP address to the tile provider.

6. Advertising and consent

Free users may see adverts supplied by Google AdMob. Eligible paid subscriptions remove AdMob adverts while the entitlement is active. Ad providers may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, consent signals and interaction data to deliver, limit, secure and measure adverts.

For users in the UK, EEA and Switzerland, MatMaps will use a Google-certified consent platform before requesting consent-dependent advertising. Personalised adverts are used only where the required consent has been obtained. On iOS, permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework is requested before tracking when required. Refusing consent does not remove free-user adverts, but may result in non-personalised or limited adverts.

You can review advertising privacy choices from the consent/privacy controls made available in the app. Learn how Google uses information for advertising at Google's Ads and Data information.

7. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share only what is needed with:

  • Google Firebase: authentication, database, file storage, cloud functions, notifications, diagnostics and app operation.
  • Apple and Google: platform sign-in, app distribution, purchases, refunds and subscription management.
  • RevenueCat: subscription entitlement and purchase-status management.
  • Google AdMob and consent/ad partners: advertising, consent, fraud prevention and measurement where advertising is enabled.
  • OpenStreetMap tile providers: delivery of the Discovery map.
  • Professional advisers, regulators, law enforcement or a purchaser of the business: only where reasonably necessary and lawful.

Current partner details shown in the in-app consent flow take precedence where advertising partners change.

8. International transfers

Some providers operate outside the UK. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we rely on an adequacy regulation, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum, contractual safeguards or another lawful transfer mechanism, as appropriate.

9. How long we keep information

We keep account information while your account is active. Content, messages, training records and club-management information are generally kept until deleted through the app, the account is deleted, or they are no longer needed for the feature. Transaction records may be retained for tax, accounting and dispute purposes. Safety, fraud, report and block records may be retained for a reasonable period to prevent repeat abuse and establish or defend legal claims.

Deletion from live systems may not remove temporary backup copies immediately. Backups are protected, cycle out under retention schedules and are not used to recreate deleted accounts except where required for disaster recovery or law.

10. Your choices and UK data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to be informed, access your information, correct it, erase it, restrict processing, receive portable information, object to processing, withdraw consent and complain. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing.

You can edit much of your profile in the app, manage permissions in device settings, block users and use the available privacy or advertising controls. Send other requests to hello@matmaps.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity and normally respond within one month.

You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. We would appreciate the opportunity to resolve the issue first.

11. Account and club-data deletion

Signed-in users can request account deletion from the profile/settings area of the app. Account deletion removes or anonymises personal profile data and associated content from active MatMaps systems, subject to limited legal, transaction, safety and backup retention described above.

Eligible club owners can separately request deletion of club data they control. Because club directory information may also come from public sources or serve other users, requests are assessed so that personal information is removed while lawful directory records and other users' rights are handled appropriately.

Deleting a MatMaps account does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel the subscription in the relevant store settings to stop future renewal.

12. Children

MatMaps is not intended for children under 13. Users aged 13 to 17 must have permission and appropriate supervision from a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from a child under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information contrary to this section.

13. Security

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including authenticated access controls and restricted administrative functions. No internet service is completely secure. Protect your account credentials and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

14. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when MatMaps, our providers or legal requirements change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice where a change materially affects your rights or choices.
