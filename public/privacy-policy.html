Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MatMaps ("MatMaps", "we", "us" or "our") collects and uses personal information when you use the MatMaps mobile app, website and related services (together, the "Services"). MatMaps is the controller responsible for the processing described in this policy.

We aim to handle personal information fairly and transparently in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and other applicable UK data protection law.

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to hello@matmaps.co.uk.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and authentication information: email address, authentication identifiers, account status and security information.
  • Profile information: name, unique @username where the account is eligible, profile image, biography, sports, ability level, belts, affiliated club and account or subscription role.
  • Friends and connections: friend requests, friendships, blocks, mentions, tags and associated timestamps.
  • Messages: one-to-one messages, message requests, delivery/read state, staff group messages and the sender attached to a club notice. Messages are private to their participants, but they are not described as end-to-end encrypted.
  • Community content: Arena posts, comments, photos, videos, likes, shares, saves, Travelling Fighter Hub text posts and reports.
  • Club and event information: club details, club @username, ownership and staff memberships, staff invitations, affiliations, member verification, notices, media, events, reviews, promotions and analytics interactions.
  • Training information: timer settings and usage, scorekeeping, training logs, notebook entries, progress statistics and coach or member notes. These entries may contain health or injury information if a user chooses to add it; users and club staff should not record sensitive information about another person without an appropriate reason and permission.
  • Location information: precise device location when permission is granted, approximate location, map searches, distance calculations and location tags added to posts or events.
  • Technical and usage information: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, identifiers, crash diagnostics, logs, pages viewed, searches and feature interactions.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers and ad impressions, clicks or related signals when AdMob advertising is enabled for eligible free users.
  • Subscription information: plan, entitlement, renewal and transaction status supplied by Apple, Google or a subscription provider such as RevenueCat when billing is connected. MatMaps does not receive or store full payment-card details from app-store purchases.
  • Support information: messages and attachments you send when contacting support, including invite-link or error details you choose to provide.

2. Where information comes from

We receive information directly from you, automatically from your device and use of the Services, from other users when they invite, tag, report, review or interact with you, and from service providers that support authentication, hosting, analytics, advertising or subscriptions.

Some club and event information is supplied by club owners, staff, users or existing data sources. Users should confirm time-sensitive club and event details directly with the organiser.

4. Public content, friends and messaging

Profile details, @usernames, Arena content, approved club reviews, public club and event details and public interaction counts may be visible to other users. Do not publish information you do not want others to see.

Friends, direct messaging and tagging are limited to eligible account types. Messages from non-friends enter Message Requests until accepted. Users may decline requests or block another account. Club pages do not have a direct inbox; a club owner or staff member communicates through their personal account.

Club notices are shown to the relevant club members. Staff group chats are restricted to the owner and staff attached to that club. A club owner controls staff invitations and membership, subject to the plan's seat limits.

5. Location information

With permission, MatMaps uses device location to centre Club Discovery, calculate proximity and order nearby results. Location tags may also be added manually to events or Travelling Fighter Hub posts. You can change location permission in your device settings, but nearby results may then be less accurate.

The in-app map uses OpenStreetMap-based mapping rather than the Google Maps SDK. An optional "Open in Google Maps" web link may open an external service governed by that provider's privacy terms.

6. Advertising and subscriptions

Eligible free accounts may see ads supplied through Google AdMob. Depending on your settings, consent and local law, ads may use an advertising identifier or may be contextual. You can use device privacy controls and any consent controls shown in the app.

When subscriptions are connected, purchases are processed by the relevant app store and may be managed through RevenueCat. Those providers supply MatMaps with entitlement and transaction status needed to unlock features. Their own privacy policies also apply.

7. Reports, reviews and moderation

Arena posts normally appear immediately. Users can report posts or comments. Reports include the reported item, reporter, selected reason or description, timestamp and review status.

Automated checks may temporarily hide obvious sexual content, spam or repeated identical posts and create a flag for human review. These checks are intended to protect the community and do not make legal or similarly significant decisions about users. An administrator may keep, restore, dismiss or remove content.

New club reviews are held for administrator approval. Only approved reviews appear publicly. Owners and staff cannot review a club they run or remove its reviews.

8. Who receives information

We disclose information only as needed to operate, protect or comply with the law. Categories of recipients may include:

  • other users, clubs and staff according to the visibility and sharing features you use;
  • Firebase and Google Cloud services used for authentication, databases, storage, notifications, analytics and crash reporting;
  • Google AdMob when advertising is enabled;
  • Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat when subscriptions are connected;
  • professional advisers, regulators, law enforcement or courts where required; and
  • a purchaser or successor if MatMaps is reorganised, sold or transferred, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information.

9. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the UK. Where required, we use a recognised adequacy regulation or appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to approved standard contractual clauses, or another lawful transfer mechanism. Contact us for more information about the safeguard relevant to your data.

10. How long we keep information

We keep account information while the account is active and retain other information only for as long as needed for the purpose collected. Retention depends on the feature, legal and accounting duties, safety and dispute needs, whether another user still needs a shared record, and our backup cycle.

Reports, moderation records, blocks, subscription records and security logs may be kept after account deletion where reasonably needed to prevent abuse, establish legal claims or comply with law. Backups are deleted or overwritten through normal retention cycles and are not used for ordinary product activity.

11. Account and club deletion

You can request account deletion through the in-app account controls or by emailing us. We will delete or anonymise profile information and, where the feature supports it, associated posts, comments, interactions and user-created events, subject to lawful retention needs and technical backup cycles.

Messages already delivered to another user and records needed to preserve a conversation, report or safety decision may remain for the recipient or be anonymised. Deleting an account does not automatically delete content another user independently created.

Eligible club owners may separately delete club data without deleting their personal account. This may remove club content, events, staff access, affiliations and related operational records. We may retain minimal records where needed to resolve disputes or comply with law.

12. Your UK data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to be informed, access your information, correct inaccurate information, erase information, restrict processing, receive portable information, object to processing, and withdraw consent.

Right to object: where we rely on legitimate interests, you may object to that processing. You may always object to direct marketing.

To exercise a right, email hello@matmaps.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity. Rights can be limited by law, including where information concerns another person.

You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern first.

13. Security and younger users

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including access controls and managed cloud services. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your password and device secure and tell us if you suspect unauthorised access.

Users must be at least 13. A user under 18 may only use MatMaps with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly seek to collect information from children who do not meet these requirements. Contact us if you believe a child's information has been collected incorrectly.

14. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as MatMaps changes or to reflect legal requirements. We will change the "Last updated" date and provide an additional notice where a change materially affects users.

Contact MatMaps about this policy, a privacy right or account deletion at hello@matmaps.co.uk.
